About

Who we are

We are the staff of Boston Bouldering Project, the climbing gym at 12A Tyler Street in Somerville, Massachusetts. Our union is made up of employees from all departments. In July 2026, we voted 42-6 in a National Labor Relations Board election to form it.

Why we formed a union

We care about this gym, and we know that sustainable working conditions make it a safer, better place for everyone. We are here every day and know what needs to improve, but as individuals we don't have the power to make those changes happen.

We are particularly focused on increasing our pay, implementing consistent safety training for staff, and creating clear pathways for growth within the company. When we ask for the changes we need together, we have a powerful voice to negotiate with the company and see real change.

Part of a national movement

We are part of Climbing Workers United, a campaign of Workers United, which is an affiliate of the Service Employees International Union (SEIU). The campaign brings together coaches, instructors, routesetters and customer-experience staff at climbing gyms across the country. Workers United now represents staff at more than 30 climbing gyms nationwide, including other Bouldering Project locations.
