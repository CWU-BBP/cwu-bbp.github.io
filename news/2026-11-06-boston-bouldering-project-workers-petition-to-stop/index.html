Boston Bouldering Project workers petition to stop retaliation

Since forming our union, we have seen what we believe is a pattern of retaliation and union-busting at Boston Bouldering Project. We have come together in a petition calling on Bouldering Project to stop.

Boston Bouldering Project employees: sign the petition.

What concerns us

  • Health insurance: an expansion of health insurance benefits is being withheld from Boston employees, even though the union has already agreed to the change.
  • Benefits and bonuses: benefits that were previously available have been paused.
  • Promotions: highly qualified, long-term employees and union leaders have been denied opportunities for promotion through hiring processes that we believe are inconsistent with past practice.

What we're asking for

We are calling on Bouldering Project to:

  • End all retaliation and interference with union activity
  • Extend the agreed-upon health insurance benefits to eligible Boston employees
  • Restore benefits and practices that have been withheld or changed
  • Ensure fair and consistent opportunities for advancement
  • Make affected employees whole
  • Bargain and engage with our union in good faith, and treat all workers fairly regardless of union involvement

Standing with the Twin Cities

We also stand with our fellow Bouldering Project worker in the Twin Cities who was fired on the day of their union election, and we call for their reinstatement. No one should be punished or intimidated for organizing.
